Biography

Dr. Charles Yang Chun is currently a Professor in the School of Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering. He has been with Nanyang Technological University since 1999. He obtained his B.Sc. degree in Air-Conditioning Engineering from the Department of Thermal Engineering of Tsinghua University, M.Sc. degree in Thermophysics from University of Science and Technology of China, and Ph.D. degree in Mechanical Engineering from University of Alberta. He is Fellow of American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME) and Asian Union of Thermal Science and Engineering (AUTSE). He received Honors of Excellent Student from Tsinghua University, Andrew Stewart Memorial Graduate Prize from University of Alberta, numerous best paper awards and research awards. He is the author and coauthor of more than 280 papers in refereed international journals. He has co-authored one textbook entitled “Elementary Electrokinetic Flow”, and 28 book chapters. He also has four US patents in his name. He had served as Editor for International Communications of Heat and Mass Transfer, Open Physics Journal, and Associate Editor for Science Bulletin and ASME Open Journal of Engineering. Currently, he is Editor-in-Chief for Frontiers in Heat and Mass Transfer, Secion Editor for Energy (Elsevier), and also on the Editorial/Advisory Boards of several journals such as Microfluidics and Nanofluidics (Springer-Verlag Publishers), Electrophoresis (Wiley), Experimental Results (Cambridge Press), Engineered Science - Energy & Environmental Journal, Journal of Thermal Science (Elsevier), Thermo (MDPI), Journal of Aerospace Engineering and Mechanics etc. He also served as a guest editor for Microfluidics and Nanofluidics, Biomicrofluidics, Electrophoresis, and ASME Journal of Heat Transfer. He was the Conference Program Chair for the 5th ASME Micro/Nanoscale Heat & Mass Transfer International Conference (2016) and the Conference Chair for the 2nd Conference on Advances in Microfluidics and Nanofluidics and the Asian-Pacific International Symposium on Lab on Chip (2011). He has been an assessor for the China Chang Jiang Scholar Award, Czech Science Foundation, Dutch Technology Foundation, Israel Science Foundation, National Science Award of China, National Science and Engineering Research Council of Canada, Netherlands Organisation for Scientific Research, Research Grant Council of Hong Kong, American Chemical Society - Petroleum Research Fund, and Research Grant Council of Australia.
