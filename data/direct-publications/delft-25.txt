Prof.dr. R. (Rene) Pecnik
Profiel

My research is focused on both, applied research in close collaboration with industries in the field of energy conversion systems, and fundamental research in the fields of numerical analysis, fluid mechanics and turbulence. In particular, my research expertise covers: 

Fundamental 
Turbulent heat transfer 
Radiative transfer
Turbulence modeling
Supercritical fluids
Unsteady turbomachinery flows 
Direct numerical simulations
High performance computing (GPGPU)
Here you can see a talk I gave for the opening of the Delft Blue Supercomputer
Applied
Turbomachinery 
Supercritical power cycles
Heat recovery and upgrading systems
