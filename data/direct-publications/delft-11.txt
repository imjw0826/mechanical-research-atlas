Profiel

Riender Happee investigates the human interaction with automated vehicles focussing on safety, comfort and acceptance.

Riender Happee is Associate Professor at

Intelligent Vehicles / Cognitive Robotics
faculty of Mechanical, Maritime & Materials Engineering (3mE)
Transport & Planning
faculty of Civil Engineering and Geosciences (CEG)

Previous employment

Technische Universität München, Lehrstuhl für Ergonomie, Visiting Professor (2015-2017).
TNO Automotive, Road Safety and Biomechanics (1992-2007).
TU Eindhoven, Lecturing Injury Biomechanics (1997-2000).
MSc in Mechanical Engineering and PhD (1992) at TU Delft.
