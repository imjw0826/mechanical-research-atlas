Biography

Dr. Liu obtained his PhD with dissertation of "Numerical simulation of large amplitude ship motions and applications to ship design and safe operation" from National Technical University of Athens in 2011.

Dr. Liu joined the School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering of Nanyang Technological University as a lecturer on December 4, 2017.

Dr. Liu's research interests fall into applied marine hydrodynamics, cover in general the seakeeping, manoeuvering, wave load, stability, safety, and their impact on the design/optimization of a ships and structures. He has been working on maritime data analytics to support maritime decarbonization. His work has been adopted by IMO guidelines, ISO standards, ITTC recommended procedures and DNV recommended practices, etc.
