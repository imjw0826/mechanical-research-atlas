K. Lisa Yang Global Engineering and Research (GEAR) Center

GEAR Center characterizes the unique technical and socioeconomic constraints of emerging markets, then combines these insights with engineering science to create high-performance, low-cost, globally-relevant technologies.

Our technologies aim to have a positive impact on the world and produce novel scientific & engineering knowledge. We focus on working within communities to elucidate design requirements, and perform field studies to evaluate efficacy.

The GEAR Center is directed by Professor Amos Winter in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at MIT.

We are looking for new graduate students!

If you are a newly admitted MIT student and are interested in any of the project descriptions below, please reach out to the corresponding postdoctoral associate!

Water Treatment in the Amazon

The project will identify, optimize, and validate existing water‑treatment technologies suited to rural communities in the Amazon Rainforest, adapting feasible low‑cost solutions to ensure long‑term, sustainable implementation and operation in remote sites. 

Contact: Leonardo Capeleto de Andrade (capeleto@mit.edu)

Making Rural Water Treatment Safer Through Smart Coagulant Control

This project aims to retrofit drinking water treatment systems by developing an operator-assistive tool for optimal coagulation, potentially improving water safety for billions in Asia.

Contact: Chotiwat Jantarakasem (choja@mit.edu) 

Designing desert-optimized greenhouses for efficient water and energy use in agriculture

This project aims to develop and validate greenhouse architectures tailored to hot, arid conditions in the UAE and MENA, reducing water and energy use while maintaining profitable and scalable crop production.

Contact: Mohamed Naouri (mnaouri@mit.edu) 

If you have ideas for other projects, feel free to reach out to our lab manager, Emily Welsh (ewelsh@mit.edu).

Watch: GEAR Center Innovations for Water and Agriculture in MENA — May 20, 2025 webinar

GEAR Center recently showcased our cutting-edge water and agriculture projects underway across the MENA region. Focused on creating solutions that can feed a growing population in the face of water stress, climate change, and energy limitations, we have realized low-cost, renewably-powered technologies in drip irrigation and desalination that dramatically cut water and energy use while being cost effective for farmers. These novel technologies include low-pressure drip emitters, smart drip irrigation systems, and time-variant desalination systems. Now it’s time to bring them to market! Learn how the Center’s pioneering approach is building resilience across the region—and how MIT alumni and friends can help drive lasting impact for communities across MENA. 

Presented by Prof. Amos Winter (Germeshausen Professor of Mechanical Engineering; GEAR Center PI), Dr. Mohamed Naouri (K. Lisa Yang GEAR Center Postdoctoral Fellow), and Fiona Grant (GEAR Center researcher, Drip Irrigation Team).
