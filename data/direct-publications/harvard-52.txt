Hekstra Lab

Welcome to the website of Prof. Doeke Hekstra's research group at Harvard University. In the Hekstra lab, we're applying the tools of physics and chemistry to illuminate biological function.

The Hekstra lab is part of the Department of Molecular & Cellular Biology and the School of Engineering & Applied Sciences
