BIO

Current position:
Reader, Dept. of Mechanical Engineering, Imperial College London (2021–present)

Education:
1. D.Phil. in Materials Science & Engineering, University of Oxford (2015)
2. B.Eng. First Class, Mechanical Engineering, University of Bath (2009)

Research impact:
1. Published 110 peer-reviewed articles (h-index 48, > 6,500 citations) 
2. Secured ~£4.5 M Research Funding
3. Inventor of open-air solid bonding technology (UK Patent 2410804.5, July 2024)

Leadership & service:
Director of Postgraduate Studies (2024–present)

Awards:
1. Leverhulme Prize (2025): For whose research had international impact and whose future research career is exceptionally promising (only 1 at Imperial and 5 in Engineering across the UK, £100k)

2.Imperial President’s Medal (2022): Outstanding Research Team, Imperial College (1 award across Imperial)
3. Imperial President’s Award (2018): Outstanding Early Career Researcher (3 awards across Imperial)
