Markus Basan Lab
Welcome to the Basan Lab
Quantitative biology on cellular and organism scales
Phenotypic diversity

The goal of our lab is to gain a quantitative and predictive understanding of biological systems, beyond individual molecular interactions and circuits, on the level of phenotypes and physiology at the cellular and organism scale. We use bacteria, cell lines and model organisms.
