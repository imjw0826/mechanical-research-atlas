BIO

Dr. Liyun (Zainab) Ma is an Assistant Professor in Advanced and Intelligent Textiles at the Hamlyn Centre and the Department of Mechanical Engineering, Imperial College London. She is also a Visiting Researcher in Professor Dame Molly Stevens’ group at the Department of Physiology, Anatomy and Genetics (DPAG), University of Oxford. Her research focuses on intelligent devices and advanced textiles for human-oriented applications through multidisciplinary, multiscale, and AI-assisted engineering approaches. These include intelligent wearable devices for monitoring applications, closed-loop diagnostic and therapeutic biomedical systems, functional and smart textiles (including electronic fibers, intelligent yarns and fabrics, silk protein materials, and biomedical textiles), augmented sensing technologies powered by AI for human-robot interaction, renewable energy harvesting devices, and large-scale fabrication and manufacturing technologies for producing textile devices.

She joined Imperial College London in 2025 as a Lecturer, following postdoctoral training in Professor Dame Molly Stevens’ laboratory at both the University of Oxford and Imperial. She earned her PhD in Textile Engineering from Donghua University in 2021, under the joint supervision of Professor Jun Wang (Donghua University), Professor Xiang Yang Liu (Xiamen University), and Professor Zhong Lin Wang (Beijing Institute of Nanoenergy and Nanosystems, CAS), with cross-institutional training at all three institutes.

Over the course of her research career, Dr. Ma has worked across seven departments—Textiles, Mechanical Engineering, Electronic Engineering, Bioengineering, Materials Science, Physics, and Physiology, Anatomy and Genetics—accumulating extensive transdisciplinary expertise. She has published over 50 high-profile peer-reviewed papers (24 as first or corresponding author) and holds 22 granted patents. She has participated in and led various research projects, including as a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Fellowship (UKRI-funded). She currently serves as a Young Editorial Board Member for five international journals and is an active peer reviewer for several leading journals in her field. In recognition of her scientific contributions and outreach efforts, she was named Highly Commended in the 2024 L’Oréal-UNESCO For Women in Science UK & Ireland Rising Talents Award.

Feel free to reach out regarding collaboration or other research opportunities. She welcomes talented and motivated students and researchers who are interested in joining her group.
