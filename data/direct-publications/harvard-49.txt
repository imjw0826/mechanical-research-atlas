Structural Geology and Earth Resources
Welcome

The Structural Geology & Earth Resources Group investigates the nature of faults, fractures, and fault-related folds in the Earth’s crust with applications to energy systems, earthquake hazards assessment, and regional tectonics.

For more information about our group and student opportunities follow this link

Latest News

October 2026: John Shaw is interviewed by NOVA to discuss what causes earthquakes, how cities and buildings can be engineered to survive them, and how human activity can trigger quakes of its own.

March 2026: Andreas Plesch, Scott T. Marshalls and John H. Shaw's paper, "The Community Fault Model (v. 6.1) for Southern California", is published in the Bulletin of Seismological Research (BSSA).

February 2026: The Statewide California Earthquake Center announces new earthquake service which is based on the Community Fault Model.

January 2026: Natasha Toghramadjian and colleagues' paper, "Probing the Seattle Basin Edge Using a Dense Urban Nodal Array in 100 Backyards," is published in SRL.

January 2026: Ben Littlejohn, Lluís Saló-Salgado, Andreas Plesch, John H. Shaw's paper, "Integrated geologic and socio-economic evaluation of potential underground hydrogen storage sites in the Los Angeles Basin," is published in the International Journal of Hydrogen Energy.

January 2026: Natasha Toghramadjian and colleagues' paper, "Deep Sources of Recent Volcanism in Armenia Inferred From Ambient Noise Tomography," is published in JGR Solid Earth.

June 2025: Robert Welch and John Shaw's paper, "Structural styles and kinematic evolution of the Front Ranges of the Southern Canadian Rockies," is published in Geosphere. 

June 2024: Kristen Chiama received a Best Student Presentation Award at the 2024 Annual Seismological Society of America (SSA) conference in Anchorage, Alaska. 

April 2024: Lydia Bailey and colleagues' paper, "Acceleration of Deep Subsurface Fluid Fluxes in the Anthropocene," is published by AGU and referenced in an article in EOS. 

April 2024: Natasha Toghramadjian and John Shaw's paper, "3D Geometry and Slip Distribution in the Long Beach Earthquake Gate, Newport–Inglewood Fault," is published in the Bulletin of the Seismological Society of America. 

November 2023: Natasha Toghramadjian was asked by Harvard Griffin GSAS News to discuss her research. 

September 2023: Natasha Toghramadjian wins the Best Overall Presentation Award at the SCEC 2023 Annual Meeting.

Current Research Topics
Wilmington fault stability

A tectonically active fault capable of generating damaging earthquakes.

Puente Hills blind thrust

A tectonically active structure that directly underlies the metropolitan area of Los Angeles.

Basement-involved wedge structures

We document the existence of a new class of structural wedge.
