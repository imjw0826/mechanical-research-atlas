Biography

Professor Mark Thompson gained his MEng in Engineering and Materials Science from the University of Oxford in 1997, followed by a PhD (Biomechanics, IRC in Biomedical Materials) from the University of London, 2001.

He is a Chartered Engineer, Member of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers and AMIM (Associate Member Institute of Materials).
