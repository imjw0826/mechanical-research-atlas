Drinking Water from From Air

A device developed by Evelyn Wang's team harvests clean drinking water from vapor in the air, even in the most arid conditions.

System can sterilize medical tools using solar heat

A device developed by graduate student Lin Zhao, Professor Evelyn Wang, and Professor Gang Chen, could provide pressurized steam to run autoclaves without the need for electricity in off-grid areas.

Simple, solar-powered water desalination
