Biography

Prof Ang Wei Tech is currently the Executive Director of the Rehabilitation Research Institute of Singapore, a joint research collaboration by NTU, A*STAR and NHG. He holds joint faculty appointments at the School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering and the Lee Kong Chian School of Medicine. Dr Ang’s research focuses on robotics technology in biomedical applications, and assistive and rehabilitation technologies. He has won many competitive research grants totaling more than S$40M. Prof Ang graduated from Carnegie Mellon University, USA with a PhD degree in Robotics in 2004, and from NTU Singapore with M.Eng. and B.Eng. degrees in Mechanical Engineering in 1999 and 1997 respectively.
