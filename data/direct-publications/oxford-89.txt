Biography

Maike Osborne is an expert in the development of intelligent algorithms capable of making sense of complex big data. Her work in Machine Learning and non-parametric data analytics has been successfully applied in diverse and challenging contexts. For example, in astrostatistics, Maike’s probabilistic algorithms have aided the detection of planets in distant solar systems, and in autonomous robotics, her work has enabled self-driving cars to determine when their maps may have changed due to roadworks. 

More recently, she has addressed key societal challenges, analysing how intelligent algorithms might soon substitute for human workers, and predicting the resulting impact on employment. Maike is an Associate Professor in Machine Learning, and an Official Fellow of Exeter College, University of Oxford.
