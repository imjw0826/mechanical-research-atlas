Biography
Biography

Andrea Vedaldi is Professor of Computer Vision and Machine Learning, and member of the VGG group at the University of Oxford.

His research focuses on computer vision and machine learning methods to understand the content of images and videos automatically, with little to no manual supervision, in terms of semantics and 3D geometry.
