Biography

Associate Professor Mir Feroskhan graduated in Aerospace Engineering from Nanyang Technological University (NTU) and received his Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering from Florida Institute of Technology. He is currently the Director of the Intelligent Cybernetics Lab (ICL), where he leads research in aerial robotics and embodied intelligence, with a focus on autonomous UAV systems and drone swarms. His research focuses on scalable autonomy, coordination, and decision-making for cooperative and heterogeneous aerial swarms, as well as robust navigation and flight control for next-generation aerial platforms.

Supported by major national grants, including the MOE Tier 2 and A*STAR Young Individual Research Grant, his research advances AI-enabled aerial robotic systems from foundational theory to real-world deployment. His expertise further extends to Air Traffic Management (ATM) and Space Situational Awareness (SSA), where he develops scalable, data-driven, and decision-centric solutions for complex aerospace environments. He currently serves as the Deputy Director of the Air Traffic Management Research Institute (ATMRI).
