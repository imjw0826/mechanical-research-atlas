Biography

Dr. Li Hongying is an Assistant Professor at the School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Nanyang Technological University (NTU). She joined NTU in April 2025. Prior to this, she spent six months at Imperial College London for overseas research exposure. She obtained her PhD in Mechanical Engineering from NTU in 2012 and subsequently joined the Institute of High Performance Computing (IHPC), A*STAR, as a Research Scientist.

Dr. Li’s research focuses on computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and its applications across various engineering fields. In recent years, she has actively integrated machine learning (ML) and artificial intelligence (AI) with CFD, including the development of surrogate models for aerosol transmission, the application of physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) for aerosol dispersion, and the use of AI libraries to solve fluid dynamics problems. Since 2012, Dr. Li has contributed to more than 30 research and industry projects spanning diverse engineering domains. Her expertise extends beyond academia, as she consistently translates research outcomes into real-world impact to drive economic growth and improve public health. Her commitment to practical applications has been instrumental in bridging the gap between scientific advancements and societal needs.

Dr. Li has authored and co-authored over 60 publications in refereed journals, including Nature Materials, The Lancet Microbe, International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer, and Applied Thermal Engineering. Her contributions have been recognized through numerous awards, such as the Technology for Enterprise Capability Upgrading Emerging Talent Award (2020), ASEAN-U.S. Science Prize for Women (2021, mid-career category), Ministry of Trade & Industry Firefly Borderless Gold Award (2022), COVID-19 Resilience Medal (2023), SG Women in Tech (2023), and Asia’s Top 50 Women Tech Leaders Award (2025).
