BIO

I am an Assistant Professor in Interfacial Fluid Mechanics and Machine Learning at the Department of Mechanical Engineering.

 

My work concerns the detection and understanding of transient and faint signals which precipate large phase changes in complex stochastic systems, for example, the how early can we predict the violent and unpredictable rupture event of a bubble. Tracking the precise lifetime of a bubble or other interfacial fluid object requires knowledge at the molecular level as well as the macroscale level which we can see with the naked eye, as well as understanding what stochastic really means.
