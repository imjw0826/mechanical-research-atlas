Biography

Dr. Changjin Huang joined Nanyang Technological University (NTU) in September 2018 as an Assistant Professor. He received his B.ENG. degree in Thermal Science and Energy Engineering from University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) in 2008, and then Ph.D. degree in Engineering Science and Mechanics from Pennsylvania State University in 2014. Before joining NTU, he worked as a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Northwestern University from 2014-2015, and then as a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Department of Biomedical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) from 2016-2018. Dr. Huang’s research interests generally lie at the intersection of mechanics, materials, engineering and biology, focusing on mechanics and manufacturing of soft and living systems.

About Us

Welcome to the webpage of Prof. Changjin Huang's research group in School of Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering (MAE) at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore!

We are a highly interdisciplinary research team dedicated to advancing the frontiers of both biomechanics and mechanics-driven manufacturing of soft systems using physical approaches, engineering tools and computational models. In the field of biomechanics, we delve into the intricate mechanics of cells and subcellular structures to uncover the fundamental principles governing their behavior and interactions. On the bio-inspired engineering front, we innovate by fabricating cutting-edge in vitro systems and bio-inspired constructs using soft materials. Leveraging advanced 3D printing techniques, we strive to mimic the complexity of natural systems, paving the way for breakthroughs in medical research and engineering applications. Our current research interests include:

Mechanics of biological systems
Transport at bio-interfaces
Mechanobiology
Mechanics of soft materials
Manufacturing of soft and living systems

More details are available on our RESEARCH page.
