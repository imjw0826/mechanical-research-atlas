BIO

Amir Kadiric is Professor of Tribology and Surface Mechanics in the Department of Mechanical Engineering. He is a member of the Tribology research group and the Director of the SKF University Technology Centre for Tribology at Imperial College.

He obtained his MEng degree in Mechanical Engineering followed by a PhD in Tribology from Imperial College in 2005. He subsequently took a post at SKF’s Engineering and Research Centre in the Netherlands where he worked on rolling bearing research, before returning to Imperial.

Prof Kadiric’s primary research areas are surface damage mechanisms in mechanical systems, including rolling contact fatigue, scuffing, fretting and hydrogen effects; reliability and efficiency of electric vehicle drivetrains; high speed tribology, and contact mechanics. He has strong links with industry and his research is applicable to a wide range of engineering systems including wind turbines, electric vehicles and aerospace applications.
