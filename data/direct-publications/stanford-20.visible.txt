"Want to Find Fulfillment at Last? Think Like a Designer"

"Stanford's Most Popular Class Isn't Computer Science—It's Something Much More Important"

The Stanford Life Design Lab welcomes Junaid Aziz as its new Managing Director. Before arriving at this role, Junaid spent a career doing what the Lab teaches: designing the experience of change, across luxury ateliers, engineering studios, diplomatic chambers, and cricket grounds on three continents. Under his leadership, the Lab will carry the work of Bill and Dave into the terrain where it is most urgently needed: the AI-centric, pressure-saturated, meaning-starved world that today's students are inheriting.

The work, as Junaid puts it, has always been the same.
