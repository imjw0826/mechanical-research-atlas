Biography

Dr Li Dong is currently a Senior Lecturer at the School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. He also serves as the Dep. Director for the MSc Supply Chain Engineering program.

He earned his BSc in Mechanical Engineering from Wuhan University of Science and Technology, an MSc in Industrial Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, and a Ph.D. in Operations Management from the Rotterdam School of Management, Erasmus University. Before joining NTU, Dr. Li Dong held roles as Research Fellow at the MIT-SUTD International Design Center (2015-2017), Assistant Professor at Lingnan College, Sun Yat-sen University (2017-2022), and Senior Scientist at A*STAR Singapore (2022-2023).

His current research focuses on the interfaces of economics and operations, with particular interests in employing optimization, game-theoretic and data-driven models to analyse optimal decisions for operations and supply chain management, new business models, and corporate sustainability strategies. His research work has been published in top-tier and leading scientific journals, including Management Science, Production and Operations Management, European Journal of Operational Research, Omega, International Journal of Production Research, etc. He serves as the referee for over ten academic journals.

Dr. Li Dong actively collaborates with industries while doing research, teaching, and consulting. He is a Chartered Member and Board Member Director of The Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport (CILT). His research work has generated real-world impact and has received important media coverage. Notably, he has designed Resilience+, a comprehensive supply chain resilience assessment tool, which has been showcased at ITAP 2023 and acknowledged by the Prime Minister's Office Singapore. Additionally, his research and insights on supply chain management have been featured by the World Economic Forum, Forture Magazine, Financial Times, and NTU.

Dr. Li Dong teaches Data Analytics, Operations Management, and Supply Chain Management courses for undergraduate, graduate, MBA, and executive programs. He has won award for his excellence in teaching, such as MAE Teacher of the Year 2025, NTU, and Excellence in Education Award, Education 2.0 Conference 2025, Dubai. Furthermore, as an advisor, he has delivered training and consultancy services to prominent companies such as State Grid, Huawei, Sam's West, ITC, Edwards Lifesciences, among others.
