Prof. Norman Fleck

Professor of Mechanics of Materials (1997)

Academic Division: Mechanics, Materials and Design

Research group: Materials Engineering (Head of Group)

Telephone: +44 1223 7 48240

Email: naf1@cam.ac.uk

Personal website

Research interests

Professor Fleck's research interests include mechanical behaviour of materials; micromechanics of deformation and fracture; fatigue, fracture and creep of engineering solids; plasticity theory and finite elements; metallic foams; powder compaction; and ferroelectrics.
