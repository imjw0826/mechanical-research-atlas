Biography

Prof. Dr.-Ing. Marvin Carl MAY received his B.Sc., M.Sc.s in Industrial Engineering and Management as well as Information Systems and Computer Science with distinction from Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) in Germany. He joined the wbk Institute of Production Science as a Research Associate, Chief Engineer and PostDoc while obtaining his Dr.-Ing. with distinction (PhD) in Mechanical Engineering from Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT). He then worked for one year as a PostDoc with Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) and just joined NTU.

His research is focused on Industrial AI, bridging the gap between theoretical and lab-focused AI and the real, industrial world which is highly domain knowledge specific, has only sparse data or suffers under giant data lakes lacking semantics and structure. A key area is in Manufacturing, spanning Design, Operations on Machine/Robotics, Systems and Network level as well as trends towards complexity. In the past 10 years he has conducted numerous project with Semiconductor Manufacturers, the Automotive Industry, Process Industry, Advanced Manufacturing among others.

Besides various awards for his research, he is a Research Affiliate in the prestigious International Academy of Production Engineering (CIRP) and has been invited to share his research in various platforms from industrial forums to scientific conferences globally. He is the General Chair of the CIRPe 2026 conference and editor or reviewer for various journals or conferences.
