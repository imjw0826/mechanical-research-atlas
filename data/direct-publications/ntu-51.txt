Biography

Sameer Alam is Director of the Air Traffic Management Research Institute (ATMRI), a joint research institute established by the Civil Aviation Authority of Singapore and Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. He is also a tenured Associate Professor in NTU’s School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering. From 2020 to 2024, he served as Co-Director of the Saab–NTU Joint Laboratory, where he led research on digital and remote air traffic control towers. He is a Co-Lead Principal Investigator for the European SESAR projects HyperSolver and DeepFlow, and a Principal Investigator under the First Executive Programme of Scientific and Technological Cooperation between Italy and Singapore, focusing on artificial intelligence.

Sameer received his PhD in Computer Science, specialising in machine learning, from the University of New South Wales (UNSW), Australia, in 2008. His postdoctoral research on artificial intelligence algorithms for advanced air traffic management concepts was supported by EUROCONTROL through a CARE Innovative Research Grant from 2009 to 2010 and by Airservices Australia through an ARC Linkage Grant from 2010 to 2011.

In 2011, he was appointed University Lecturer, equivalent to Assistant Professor, at UNSW Canberra and was promoted to Senior Lecturer, equivalent to Associate Professor, in 2015. He joined NTU Singapore as an Associate Professor in Aerospace Engineering in 2017 and was granted tenure in 2023. He has also served as a visiting scientist at NASA Ames Research Center, California, in 2011 and at École Nationale de l’Aviation Civile (ENAC), Toulouse, France, from 2016 to 2017.

His research focuses on applications of artificial intelligence and machine learning to air traffic management and airport operations. His expertise spans human–AI teaming, conformal automation, multi-agent systems, deep reinforcement learning, computer vision, and learning under uncertainty. He has authored or co-authored more than 200 peer-reviewed publications in leading journals and international conferences.

Sameer has served as an Associate Editor of the IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence since 2023 and the IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems since 2026. He has been a member of the Editorial Board of Elsevier’s Transportation Research Part C: Emerging Technologies since 2018 and served as an Associate Editor for the 2024 IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Conference. He is also a member of the Executive Committee of the FAA–EUROCONTROL U.S./Europe Air Traffic Management Research and Development Seminar and has served on the Programme Committee of the SESAR Innovation Days since 2024.

His research has received several prestigious awards, including:

ANU Science Medal, Australian National University, 2011 Fresh Science Award, Science in Public, Australia, 2009 Young Tall Poppy Science Award, Australian Institute of Policy and Science, 2011 More than 12 Best Paper Awards at major conferences, including the ATM R&D Seminar, ICNS, ICRAT, SESAR Innovation Days, and DASC

Sameer has served as a technical consultant to ICAO’s Middle East Regional Monitoring Agency from 2013 to 2024, the Ministry of Transportation and Telecommunications of the Kingdom of Bahrain from 2015 to 2024, and Saab Singapore from 2022 to 2024.

He has also contributed extensively to academic and professional leadership. His roles have included Organising Chair of the IEEE International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics in Singapore in 2020; Organising Chair of the IEEE Aviation Innovation Conference for Asia-Pacific; Co-Chair of the 28th Australasian Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Canberra; and Co-Chair of the 11th International Conference on Research in Air Transportation in Singapore in 2024. He has also chaired and served on the programme committees of numerous international conferences.

Beyond his academic and professional work, Sameer is an aviation enthusiast who enjoys general aviation flying, fishing, and mountain hiking.
