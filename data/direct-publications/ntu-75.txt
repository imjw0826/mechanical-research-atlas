Biography

Dr. Jianfei Yang joined NTU MAE jointly with EEE as an assistant professor in September 2024. Previously, he was a visiting research fellow at Harvard University and the University of Tokyo in 2024, a Presidential Postdoctoral Fellow at NTU from 2021 to 2023, and a senior research engineer at BEARS, the University of California Berkeley in 2021. He obtained his Ph.D. degree from NTU in 2021 with the Best Thesis Award and his B.Eng. degree from Sun Yat-Sen University in 2016.

Dr. Yang's research is focused on Physical AI and Embodied AI, driven by a central question: How can robots learn and interact with the world as robustly and adaptively as humans? To address this challenge, he develops multimodal embodied AI systems and feedback learning frameworks that enable robots to understand physical interactions and acquire complex skills. His work spans the full robotics stack, including multimodal perception, world models, robot learning, decision making, and real-world deployment. Through the MARS Lab, he aims to advance Physical AI systems that can continuously learn, adapt, and operate effectively in complex real-world environments.

Dr. Yang has been recognized in the Forbes Asia 30 Under 30 List in 2024 and the World’s Top 2% Scientists List compiled by Stanford University since 2022. He serves as an Associate Editor for two flag AI journals, IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems, and Neurocomputing. Dr. Yang is keen on participating in AI and data challenges, winning 1st place in over 10 academic or industrial international AI challenges.
