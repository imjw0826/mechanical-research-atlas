Biography

Professor Thomas Povey is a Professor of Engineering Science and a Tutorial Fellow of University College, where he is also the Tacchi Fellow in Engineering Science. He completed a degree in Physics at St Catherine’s College Oxford, followed by a DPhil in Engineering Science (Turbomachinery) in the Rolls-Royce University Technology Centre in Heat Transfer and Aerodynamics at Oxford University. His supervisor was Professor Terry Jones.

Following his DPhil, Tom was appointed as the Rolls Royce Industrial Fellow at St Catherine’s College before taking up his current post as Tutorial Fellow at University College in 2004. In 2008 he took up the Tacchi Fellowship in Engineering Science, and in 2014 became a Professor of Engineering Science. In 2024 he was elected a Fellow of the Royal Aeronautical Society. His research group pioneered accurate metal effectiveness measurement techniques, ultrahigh-accuracy engine capacity measurement techniques, and high frequency (10 KHz) cooling modulation methods by acoustic optimisation of cooling holes. Tom has published about 130 peer-reviewed papers in turbomachinery conferences and journals, with an emphasis on turbine performance, heat transfer, and combined aerothermal problems. Tom has 12 granted patents in gas turbine technology and flow control technologies. He has supervised 24 DPhil students.

Tom is a founder director of the University spin-out company Oxford Flow, a manufacturer of high-performance pressure regulators for the water, oil and gas industries. Oxford Flow has won many industry awards for valve technology, particularly for zero-emissions valves. In 2015 Tom wrote a mathematics and physics book (Professor Povey’s Perplexing Problems), aimed at the 16–18 (pre-university) age group. This has sold over 40,000 copies with translations into Chinese, Korean, and Japanese.
