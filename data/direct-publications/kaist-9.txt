iCaRE Lab.
in-situ Characterization and Reliability Evaluation Laboratory

극한소재역학 및 신뢰성평가 연구실 

Our research interests span the areas of mechanics and materials. 
We aim to understand the mechanical behavior of materials at different length scales, environment conditions and to use the knowledge to develop materials and structures for advanced technological applications.
