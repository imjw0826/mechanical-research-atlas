YJ GROUP

Our research group investigates the design, processing, and mechanics of advanced biomaterials, spanning both synthetic and biological systems. We aim to uncover the fundamental principles governing large deformations and self-healing behaviors, while translating these insights into practical engineering solutions, such as soft machines, artificial muscles, biohybrid robots, and biomedical devices.
