Biography

Professor Stephen Roberts has a first degree in Physics and a DPhil in machine learning. He was faculty at Imperial College, London prior to his appointment in Oxford in 1999.

He leads the Machine Learning Research Group, is a Professorial Fellow of Somerville College, founding Director of the EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training in Autonomous, Intelligent Machines and Systems (AIMS), co-Director of the Oxford ELLIS unit and was Director of the Oxford-Man Institute from 2015-2021.

Stephen is Co-Founder and Scientific Advisor of Mind Foundry, PI of Oxford's Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellowship programme and co-I of the Intelligent Earth Centre for Doctoral Training.
