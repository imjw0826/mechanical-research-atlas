Centre Projects

AM.NUS works closely with the academia and industry on both upstream fundamental research in additive manufacturing as well as translational projects. We apply our capabilities in artificial intelligence, functional printing, advanced materials research, simulation, design and AM processes in the three main sectors below, namely Advanced Manufacturing, Health Science and Sustainability.
