Biography

Eddie obtained:
B.Eng (CL I) from Uni. of Newcastle upon Tyne;
Ph.D at Cambridge Univ. with a Cambridge Commonwealth Scholarship;
PG Diploma in Teaching Higher Edu., NIE-NTU.

He is elected as:
Fellow, Singapore Academy of Engineering Sciences (https://www.aessuav.org/).
Academician for European Academy of Sciences (EUAS, Greece), composed of the world’s leading scientists, Nobel prize and outstanding scientists winners, scholars and business people, aiming to promote excellence in science and technology (see pp. 199-202 in http://www.interpaper.org/2022-Annual-Report-EU-Academy-of-Sciences.pdf and pp. 67 to 71 on Annual 2023 EUAS in http://www.interpaper.org/MEMBERS.htm on his unique contributions in non-contact, early-detection of human-health abnormality non-invasively with infrared-physics-technology: a novel 'tissue-activities-ratio analysis' method).
Fellow (inaugural) for National Academy of Technology (FNAT, USA),
NAT fellows are elected "through a highly selective, peer-review process, based on their track records of scientific achievement and original contributions that have advanced technology." His work focuses on the development of advanced contactless diagnostic medical imaging techniques based on active dynamics thermography.
Academician for European Academy of Sciences and Arts (AEASA, EU-Austria), an honor reserved for the world's most outstanding scholars;
Fellow of the American Institute for Medical and Biological Engineering (FAIMBE, USA) with his citation reads: "For outstanding contributions in non-contact, early detection of human-health abnormality non-invasively with infrared-physics-technology"，see details in https://aimbe.org/college-of-fellows/cof-9090/ ;
Fellow (Life) of the American Society of Mechanical Engineers (FASME, USA); ASME fellows are elected from top in the field members from the USA and other regions outside America.
Fellow of Institute of Engineering and Technology (FIET, United Kingdom);
Fellow of Industry Academy: the International Artificial Intelligence Industry Alliance (HK), https://www.aiia-ai.org/nd.jsp?id=909
Fellow of International Engineering and Technology Institute (FIETI, Hong Kong),
Fellow of Asia-Pacific Technological Sciences Academy (FAPTSA, Hong Kong, http://www.aptsa.org/fellows.html)
Distinguished Fellow for Institute of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence,
(DFIDSAI, China),
Member (Life) of Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence (MCAAI, China) and,
Academician for Academy of Pedagogy and Learning, (USA).
Eddie received 2022 Alumni Highly Commended Award from the VC of Newcastle upon Tyne University, UK for his contributions to the society and achievements as academia.
Eddie was named the winner of the 2024 2nd IETI (International Engineering and Technology Institute) Ramesh Agarwal Lifetime Achievement Award in Science, Engineering or Technology. This award honors individuals with lifetime achievements in research, education, and service towards the advancement of any discipline of engineering and technology. The award committee quoted that the reason for his award is: For outstanding contributions in non-invasive, contactless, early detection of human health abnormality economically with infrared physics technology & advanced AI novel analysis (https://www.ieti.net/news/detail.aspx?id=351).

He published > 642 papers in SCI-IF int. journal (500); int. conf. proceedings (148), textbook chapters (>106) and others (32) over the 28 years. Co-edited 14 books on “Cardiac Pumping and Perfusion Engineering” by WSP (2007); “Imaging and Modelling of Human Eye” by Artech (2008); “Distributed Diagnosis and Home Healthcare, v.1” by ASP (2009); “Performance Evaluation in Breast Imaging, Tumor Detection & Analysis” by ASP (2010); “Distributed Diagnosis and Home Healthcare, v.3” by ASP (2011); “Computational Analysis of Human eye with Applications” by WSP (2011); “Human Eye Imaging and Modeling” by CRC (2011); “Multimodality Breast Imaging” by SPIE (2013); “Image Analysis and Modeling in Ophthalmology”; “Ophthalmology Imaging and Applications” by CRC (2013, 2014); “Bio-inspired Surfaces and Applications” by WSP (2016); “Application of Infrared to Biomedical Sciences” by Springer (2017) and “Computation and Mathematical Methods in Cardiovascular Physiology” by WSP (2019). Also, co-authored a text book: “Compressor Instability with Integral Methods” by Springer (2007).

He is in the Stanford list of the World’s top 2% Scientists since 2019 (ranked 83 out of 64,425 as top 0.001% in the field of Biomedical Engineering, 2020) [see Website: https://elsevier.digitalcommonsdata.com/datasets/btchxktzyw/3]. The ranking method is calculated by removing the self-citation of the literature.

Ranked # 8 (Worldwide) in Google Scholar under Biomedical, cited by 23,800+ (H-index: 77+) [see http://scholar.google.com.sg/citations?user=9QW1LYAAAAAJ ]

His Scores in researchgate.net is also one of the highest within the schools and is higher than 98% of all ResearchGate members’ scores. See https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Eddie-Ng-3/scores

Additionally, he ranks 18th among the top 100 scholars in the "Top Engineering and Technology Scientists in Singapore" discipline, according to the international academic website Research.com, one of the major websites for Engineering and Technology research offering credible data on scientific contributions since 2014. [see https://research.com/scientists-rankings/engineering-and-technology/sg ]

More details like the latest H-index, SCI-total citations are available in https://www.webofscience.com/wos/author/record/309198.

Journal of Medical Systems.

His main area of research is thermal imaging, human physiology, biomedical engg; computational turbomachinery aerodynamics; micro-scale cooling problems; computational fluid dynamics & numerical heat transfer. As of 2026, he has graduated 28 Ph.D. and 27 M.Eng. research students. One of his graduated PhD students, Dr. Saxena received the NTU’s Graduate College Research Excellence Award (2020/21) and another one, Dr Tan JH is listed as the Highly Cited Researchers by Clarivate Web of Science in 2020.

He is an invited keynotes speaker for more than 35 international scientific confs./workshops. He is a Technical Program Committee Chair, “The China Physiological Signal Challenge 2021-23 cum International Conf. on Biomed. Engg & Biotechnology” (Since 2012 – present), China. His editorial efforts for a Special issue collection: “Biomedical Advances in Cancer Detection, Diagnosis, and Treatment”: A Selection of Papers from the 11th to 15th International Conference on Biomedical Engineering and Biotechnology (ICBEB 2022 to 2026): “Technology in Cancer Research & Treatment”: SAGE Journal (Good SCI-IF) as guest editor, see https://journals.sagepub.com/page/tct/collections/call-for-papers/biomedical-advances-in-cancer-detection-diagnosis-treatment . He is a General Chair of 3rd International Symposium on Big Data and Artificial Intelligence (ISBDAI 2022): see https://www.isbdai.org/committees.html , participated in recent two Xiangjiang Nobel Forums in Hong King and as Advisor for academic committee of Hong Kong World Youth Science Conference. https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202604/12/P2026041200259p.htm

Eddie is also a guest editor on: "The Role of Thermography in Cardiovascular Diseases" for Reviews in Cardiovascular Medicine: https://www.imrpress.com/journal/rcm/special_issues/1637633458398 ; Special Issue in Sensors: "Infrared/Thermal Imaging for Biomedical Applications": https://www.mdpi.com/journal/sensors/special_issues/80A5152QDH .

Twenty-one of his thermal imaging papers have been adopted as references in both Singapore Standard (SS 582: 2020) and ISO/TR 13154:2009 / IEC 80601-2-59: 2017. He was a panel member for the Biomedical and Health Standards Committee (2011-2020), Singapore. He received the SPRING Singapore Merit Award (2016) for his contributions to the Singapore Standardisation Programme. Lately, he presented “Applied Thermography for fever identification and updates on Infrared with AI for other health abnormalities: facts & myths in reflecting deeper metabolic activity”, for UNESCO World Metrology Day 2026.

The affable adjunct NUH scientist has a special interest in infrared (IR) physics & technology and has contributed greatly to research on IR, in particular on application of IR to biomedical sciences. The co-inventor of 3 USA patents on multiple analytical software classifier programs to identify the different stages of breast cancer development using thermal data with Lifeline’s First Warning TM system, he further explores the use of IR in the field of ophthalmology for early detection of health abnormality. Those 3 patents have attracted more than USD10m from USA directly. This First Warning Systems are processed for the CE Mark currently & to be followed by clinical validation with FDA and market entry of the inventions in select European markets. He collaborates with fellow researchers from National Health Group Hospitals, also, Poland and USA so as to consistently achieve breakthrough such as concluding the possibility of using the Biofield Diagnostic System as a better objective assessment tool for complimentary breast cancer detection.

He is granted a patent (AusPat: 2021101042) in “loT enabled EPCG device unit for nursing heart health distantly with Artificial intelligence at cloud-based environment”. He received the Engineering and Technology [UK] Innovations Awards 2020 for the Model-based Engineering Competition. Finalist (selected out from a total of 300 entries) in category for the ‘Excellence in Model-Based Engineering’. Highly Commended by all the 6 panel judges- International awards ceremony, London. He is the lead for project "AI in wind farms" with Western Sydney University and showcases the passion, commitment and innovation in engineering and technology today.

Overview

As an annual gathering since 2012, ICBEB aims to provide a forum for academic experts and participants to exchange their experiences and share research results in the fields of Cell Biology, Bioinformatics & Oncology, Biomechanics & Biomedical Modeling, Medicinal Chemistry, Medical Imaging Technology and Application, Biomedical Signal Processing and Biomedical Engineering, etc., and opens doors for discussing the practical challenges and recommending better solutions for human health.

The past 10 events have featured oral and poster presentations by researchers from over 20 countries including USA, UK, Switzerland, Germany, Singapore, Israel, Sweden, Denmark, Italy, Romania, Slovenia, Belgium, Serbia, Iran, Malaysia, South Korea, Japan, India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, and Thailand.

The 11th International Conference on Biomedical Engineering and Biotechnology (ICBEB 2022) will be in-person in Shenzhen, China, and online November 15-18, 2022. This Special Collection will feature articles from attendees of ICBEB 2022 that report on novel applications, design, basic research, and other aspects related to the use of biomedical engineering and biotechnology to detect, diagnose, or treat cancer. Research papers covering all aspects of biomedical engineering and biotechnology as they relate to cancer will be welcomed. For submission of review papers, it is suggested to consult with the Guest Editor in advance.



The submitted manuscripts for this Special Collection will be peer-reviewed before publication.

For ICBEB Conference Attendees: This Special Collection is open only to attendees of ICBEB 2022. Attendees are eligible for a 20% article processing charge (APC) discount if the manuscript is accepted for publication. When submitting, please indicate this in the cover letter and select the name of the Special Collection from the Special Collection question drop-down on the first page of the submission form.

International Conference on Biomedical Engineering and Biotechnology (ICBEB) is a platform for scholars, engineers, and scientists to present robust research demonstrating the expanding frontiers in all fields of biomedical engineering and biotechnology. It works on promoting the interdisciplinary collaborations and provides the most up-to-date research in a wide variety of application fields.

Dr. Eddie Y. K. Ng, from Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, is serving as Technical Program Committee Chair and anchored Keynote Speaker for the last 10 years’ ICBEB conference series since 2012. He has given strong support and commitment to the development and advancement of ICBEB to be an established and well-known conference in Asia Pacific region.

Dr. Ng is elected as Academician for European Academy of Sciences and Arts (EASA, EU), Fellow of the American Society of Mechanical Engineers (FASME, USA) and Fellow of Institute of Engineering and Technology (FIET, United Kingdom) etc. He has published numerous papers in SCI-IF international journal (430); international conference proceedings (130), textbook chapters (>105) and others (32) over the past 29 years. He has co-edited 14 books in STEM areas. He is in the Stanford list of the World’s top 2% Scientists since 2019 (ranked 173 as 0.001% in the field of Biomedical Engineering). He is ranked # 6 (Worldwide) in Google Scholar under Biomedical, cited by 14,504 (h-index: 62). He is also the lead editor of a number of referred international journals.
