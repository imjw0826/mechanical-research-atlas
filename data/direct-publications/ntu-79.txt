Biography

Prof. Zhou is a Professor in the School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. He received his bachelor’s and master’s degrees in mechanical engineering from Tsinghua University, China, and his Ph.D. from Nanyang Technological University. His research focuses on mechanics of materials and additive manufacturing. He served as Associate Chair (Research) in the School from 2023 to 2026. He was named a Clarivate Highly Cited Researcher in Cross-Field in 2022 and 2023 and in Materials Science in 2024 and 2025. He received the 2026 J. N. Reddy Medal in Mechanics of Advanced Materials and Structures.

He is a Fellow of the Royal Society, a Foreign Member of Academia Europaea, a Fellow of the European Academy of Sciences, and a Member of the European Academy of Sciences and Arts. He was also elected a Fellow of the American Physical Society, the Institute of Physics (UK), the Institution of Mechanical Engineers (UK), the Royal Aeronautical Society (UK), the Royal Society of Chemistry (UK), and the Institute of Materials, Minerals & Mining (UK). He serves as Chair of the Singapore Adhering Organization to the International Union of Theoretical and Applied Mechanics.
