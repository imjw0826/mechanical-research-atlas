Biography

Dominic O’Brien is a Professor of Engineering Science at Oxford and leads the optical communications group. He is Director of the EPSRC funded Hub for Quantum Computing via Integrated and Interconnected Implementations (QCi3). He also leads the EPSRC Future Telecoms Hub in All-Spectrum Connectivity. His research is in the area of optical wireless communications.
