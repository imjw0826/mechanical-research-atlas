Prof.dr.ir. A.C. (Alfred) Schouten
Profiel
Expertise

My research focuses on methods and devices to assess and understand neuromuscular control in both healthy subjects and patients with neurological dysfunction. The key concept is the design of force-controlled robotic manipulators, which allow for natural movement, neuromuscular modelling, and closed-loop system identification techniques to untangle the human motor control system.
