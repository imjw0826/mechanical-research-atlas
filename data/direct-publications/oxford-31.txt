Biography Research Recognition and Awards Publications
Career

2000-Present: Donald Pollock Professor of Chemical Engineering, Oxford University

2000-Present: Professorial Fellow of Hertford College, Oxford

2001-2020 – Founding Director, Oxford Centre for Tissue Engineering and Bioprocessing

2005-2010 – Changjiang Visiting Professor, Dalian University of Technology, China

2004 – Braun Intertec Visiting Professor, University of Minnesota, USA

Oct 1999 – 2000: Reader in Engineering Science, Oxford University

Jan 1999 - Sep 1999 – Visiting Professor, Georgia Institute of Technology, USA

Oct 1994 – Sep 2000: Official Fellow and Engineering Tutor, Keble College, Oxford

Oct 1994 – Sep 1999: University Lecturer in Engineering Science, Oxford University

Apr 1991 – Sep 1994: Lecturer, Department of Chemical Engineering, University of Edinburgh, UK

May 1990 – Mar 1991: Research Fellow, Department of Pharmacy, University of Strathclyde, UK

Oct 1988 – Apr 1990: Research Associate, Bioengineering Unit, University of Strathclyde, UK

Education and Qualifications

Oct 1978 – Jul 1982 : BSc in Chemical Engineering,1982, Inner Mongolia Polytechnic University (China)

Sep 1982 – Dec 1985: MSc in Chemical Engineering, 1984, Dalian University of Technology (China)

Jan 1986 – Dec 1988:  PhD in Chemical Engineering,1987, Dalian University of Technology (China)

MA, 1994, Keble College, Oxford

DSc, 2009, University of Oxford

CSci, 2004, The Science Council, UK

CEng, 1997, The Engineering Council, UK

Fellow, Institution of the Chemical Engineers (FIChemE), 2003

Fellow, American Institute for Medical and Biological Engineering, 2014

Other Roles

Editor-in-Chief: Bio-Design and Manufacturing

Trustee: Oxford China Scholarship Fund (COSF)

Talks

Webinar on Rapid Testing Kits and International Collaboration
