Biography

Dr Tay Yi Wei Daniel is an Assistant Professor in the School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. He holds a PhD in Mechanical Engineering from NTU and was previously a visiting scholar at the Pacific Earthquake Engineering Research Center at the University of California, Berkeley, where he worked on 3D printing applications in construction and earthquake testing of structures.

His research focuses on 3D concrete printing and construction additive manufacturing, with particular interests in sustainable and recycled materials, carbon capture and sequestration, and AI-assisted material design and process monitoring. His work examines how material behaviour and printing parameters influence the performance of printed components, while exploring computer vision and automation to improve printing reliability. Through these efforts, he aims to reduce material waste, improve construction productivity, and support more resource-efficient construction practices.

Dr Tay serves as a Scientific Editor for Results in Engineering and a member of the Additive Manufacturing Technical Committee. As a member of the Construction Additive Manufacturing Working Group for the Built Environment, he contributed to the development of Singapore’s Technical Reference TR 146:2025 on 3D Concrete Printing. His research combines engineering fundamentals with practical construction needs to advance sustainable and increasingly autonomous construction methods.
