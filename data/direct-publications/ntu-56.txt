Biography

Dr. Jian-Jun SHU is on the Stanford University's list of the world's top 2% scientists, a Top Five Scientist in field of natural/mathematical sciences at Nanyang Technological University (World Scientist and University Rankings), and was a recipient of the British Institution of Mechanical Engineers 1992 BFPA Prize for Young Engineers. He is a member of the editorial boards of the journals of (Nature) Scientific Reports and Mathematical Problems in Engineering.
