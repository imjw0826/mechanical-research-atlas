Welcome to the GSTPG Lab @ NUS

About us:

We are a research group led by Prof. Wenming Yang. Our major area of research is in combustion, whereby we focus on four different aspects and applications:

1. Combustion process and emissions control of internal combustion (IC) engines fueled by various fuels such as biodiesel, syngas, methanol, ethanol, marine oil, fossil diesel and gasoline.

2. Development of the next generation IC engines with high efficiency and nearly zero soot and NOx emissions.

3. Combustion optimization and emissions control in four types of typical boilers: grate biomass boiler, pulverized coal boiler, CFB boiler as well as incinerator.

4. Microscale combustion and micro thermophotovoltaic power generators.

5. Test.
