Biography

Guo Zhan Lum received his B.Eng. with first class honors in mechanical engineering from Nanyang Technological University in 2010. He went on to pursue his postgraduate studies in mechanical engineering under the dual Ph.D. program of Nanyang Technological University and Carnegie Mellon University. He received his M. Sc. degree from Carnegie Mellon University in 2015, and dual Ph.D. degrees in 2016. From May 2016 to December 2017, he was a post-doctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems in Germany. Since 2018, he has joined Nanyang Technological University as a faculty member and he is now a tenured Associate Professor. Lum had also served as the School's Assistant Chair for International Engagement from 2022 to 2023. His research interests include soft robots, miniature robots and magnetic actuation.

To date, he has published 21 journal papers, including multi-disciplinary journals such as Nature (1x), PNAS (1x), Advanced Materials (7x) and Science Advances (1x). In 2025, his Nature paper has been conferred the prestigious Frontiers of Science Award administered by the International Congress of Basic Science. One of his works was nominated for the Best Paper Award at the 2014 Robotics: Science and Systems conference [1] and he also won the Best Paper Award at the 2010 IEEE STUDENT conference based on his undergraduate research. Over the years, his research has attracted substantial attention from the international media. For example, his Nature article, entitled “Small-scale soft-bodied robot with multimodal locomotion”, has been reported by at least 38 news outlets worldwide, including The New York Times and The Wall Street Journal [2]. His Advanced Materials article, entitled “Magnetic Miniature Soft Robot with Reprogrammable Drug‐Dispensing Functionalities: Toward Advanced Targeted Combination Therapy", has also been reported by at least 50 news outlets worldwide, including The BBC [3]. Another one of his Advanced Materials article, entitled “Small‐Scale Magnetic Actuators with Optimal Six Degrees‐of‐Freedom”, has also been reported by at least 26 news outlets worldwide [4]. In 2026, his Advanced Materials article, entitled “Miniature soft robot with magnetically reprogrammable surgical functions”, has also been reported by at least 50 news outlets worldwide, including La Razón, CCTV, National Geographic, The Straits Times, Channel News Asia and Lianhe Zaobao [5]. His research has been featured as the Front Cover [6], Inside Back Cover [7] and Frontispiece [8] of Advanced Materials. Likewise, his research has also been featured as the Front Cover [9] and Inside Back Cover [10] of Advanced Intelligent Systems.

Guo Zhan Lum had served as the Finance Chair for the 6th IEEE-RAS International Conference on Soft Robotics [11] and one of the Regional Chairs in the 2024 IEEE/SICE International Symposium on System Integration [12]. He is an Associate Editor for the 2023-2026 IEEE/RJS International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS), 2024 IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2024-2026 RoboSoft and the Journal of Micro-Bio Robotics [13]. In 2020, he was also a Guest Editor for a special issue of Advanced Intelligent Systems [14]. His expertise in soft robotics has also allowed him to contribute an expert opinion for Nature [15]. He is an active reviewer for Nature, Advanced Materials, Nature Electronics, Nature Communications, Science Advances, Science Robotics, Advanced Functional Materials, Advanced Science, Advanced Electronic Materials, Advanced Intelligent Systems, Robotics: Science and Systems, Soft Robotics, IEEE TRO, IEEE TMech, JMPS, Precision Engineering, Mechanism and Machine Theory, IEEE Industrial Electronics, IEEE TASE, IEEE MRB, Extreme Mechanics Letters, IEEE RAL, ICRA, IROS, AIM, PLOS One, Sensors, and Frontiers in Robotics and AI, etc.
