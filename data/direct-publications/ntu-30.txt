Biography

Dr. Zhi Jun Lim joined the school on 1 March 2026 as Lecturer in Aviation.

Dr. Lim received her Bachelor’s degree in Aerospace Engineering and Economics from Nanyang Technological University (NTU) in 2019 under a double degree programme. She later received her Ph.D. from Nanyang Technological University, where her research focused on data-driven modelling and artificial intelligence applications in air traffic management. Prior to joining MAE as a lecturer, she was a Research Fellow at the Air Traffic Management Research Institute (ATMRI), leading and contributing to multiple research projects on intelligent airspace operations and next-generation arrival management systems in collaboration with industry and regulatory partners.

Her research lies at the intersection of aerospace engineering, operations research, and artificial intelligence. She works on developing machine learning and reinforcement learning methods to enhance air traffic flow management, terminal manoeuvring area operations, and decision support tools for air traffic controllers. Her work integrates operational data, human factors, and AI-enabled optimisation to improve safety, efficiency, and sustainability in aviation systems. She has received recognition at international conferences and actively collaborates with global research partners across academia, industry, and aviation authorities.

At MAE, Dr. Lim aims to advance intelligent aviation systems research while fostering strong industry collaboration and mentoring the next generation of engineers and researchers. As an alumnus of NTU MAE, she completed her undergraduate studies in Aerospace Engineering (Class of 2019) and is grateful for the foundation the School provided. She is particularly motivated to contribute back to the MAE community in a faculty capacity by nurturing and inspiring future engineers, while bridging fundamental research with real-world deployment to support Singapore’s vision of next-generation airspace management and sustainable aviation.
