Biography

Professor Chan leads hydrogen and fuel cell research at Energy Research Institute at NTU (ERI@N). He is currently the Senior VP of China-Singapore International Joint Research Institute in Guangzhou Knowledge City, China.

Prof Chan obtained his PhD from Imperial College London. He is a Professor in the School of MAE and Cheng Tsang Man Chair Professor in Energy. He is a Fellow of Academy of Engineering, Singapore. He has served as a non-executive director of Maz Energy between 2014 and 2022 and spun out Xin Xiang (Guangzhou) Hydrogen Technologies in 2018.

His research has gained him several recognitions, which include George-Stephenson Medal from UK IMechE in 2000, Outstanding Scientific Achievement Award from International Association of Hydrogen Energy, USA in 2007, “World’s Most Influential Scientific Minds” award from Thomson Reuters in 2014, Nanyang Award (Research Excellence) in 2014, Nanyang Award (Innovation and Entrepreneurship) in 2017, “Star of Innovation Talent” in 2018 from Guangzhou Government, and President's Chair in Energy between 2018 and 2023. He is the recipient of two National Day Awards in 2017 and 2018. He is the editorial board member of Fuel Cells, Journal of Power Technologies, Energy Conversion and Management and International Journal of Energy Research.

Prof Chan has been teaching Thermodynamics course since 1991 and awarded “Teacher-of-the-Year” in 2000.

Prof Chan has published 382 refereed journal papers with a total citation count of more than 23,000 and h-index of 74. More recently, ScholarGPS recognized him as a Highly Ranked Scholar (Lifetime Achievement) in Mechanical Engineering and Fuel Cell, Hydrogen Fuel & Energy, placing him in the top 0.05% of scholars worldwide for exceptional productivity, impact, and quality of work.

Prof. Chan is an ardent hydrogen economy advocate for more than 30 years and he was the organizer / conference chair of the 1st World Hydrogen Technologies Convention (WHTC) held in Singapore in 2005. He is the Conference Chair of the 25th World Hydrogen Energy Conference (WHEC) www.whec2026.org to be held in Jun 22-26, 2026 at Marina Bay Sands, Singapore.
