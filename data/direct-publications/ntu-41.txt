Biography

Daniel New is currently an Associate Professor at the School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering (MAE) and Associate Dean (Undergraduate) at the College of Engineering, Nanyang Technological University. He is also the founding Programme Director of MSc (Aviation) programme, as well as the coordinator of Double-Degree programme at the School of MAE. He graduated with B.Eng. (Hons) from the National University of Singapore (NUS) in 1998 and intrigued by fundamental fluid flow phenomena, he pursued his M.Eng. in 1998 and subsequently graduated with a PhD degree specializing in fluid flows in 2004. In 2001, he joined Temasek Laboratories at NUS (TL@NUS) as an Associate Scientist and subsequently as a Research Scientist, working on flow control problems surrounding jet-mixing enhancing and control, skin friction drag reduction and flow past slender-shaped bodies. After obtaining his PhD degree, he worked on pulse detonation engine technology at the University of Texas, Arlington (UTA) as part of a post-doctoral research collaboration between TL@NUS and the Aerodynamics Research Center of UTA. He later joined the University of Liverpool, UK as a Lecturer in the School of Engineering in 2005 and focused on jet-mixing enhancement strategies with the UK MoD Defense Science and Technology Laboratory. He had also secured grants from UK EPSRC and Royal Society as part of a broader study into the fundamental vortex dynamics and characteristics of modified jet nozzles. In 2010, he joined the Division of Aerospace Engineering, School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at Nanyang Technological University, specializing in experimental/numerical investigations on both Aerodynamics and Fluid dynamics problems. Since then, he has been awarded research grants from DSO, MINDEF, FSTD, MOE, MPA and NRF, among others, as well as been visiting professorships to ONERA, France and Monash University, Australia in 2018 and 2019.

He was the Associate Chair (Students) at School of MAE from 2016 to 2018 and enrolled into NTU Leadership Academy in 2017. Over the years, he is involved in School Search Committees and Accreditation Committees, as well as taking up Faculty Advisor roles for Aerospace Society (till 2024) and Mecatron student interest groups. Additionally, he was the receipient of Tan Chin Tuan Research Fellowships in Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University Excellent Young Researcher Exchange Fellowship and more recently, Shanghai Jiao Tong University International Visiting Fellowship. For teaching, he was awarded Honoured MAE Faculty for Inspirational Teaching as part of the Koh Boon Hwee Scholars Award in 2014, 2018 and 2022. His undergraduate and research students have also won student awards at the 31st International Symposium on Shock Waves, Asian Symposium of Visualization and 21st International Symposium on Flow Visualization conferences previously.

He is a Fellow of the Royal Aeronautical Society, UK and Chartered Engineer (Aerospace Engineering) of The Institution of Engineers, Singapore. He specializes in both fundamental and applied Aerodynamics and Fluid Dynamics research, including topics such as but not lmited to vortex ring and jet flow phenomena, hydrodynamic flows, flow separation control, lift enhancement and drag reduction, supersonic jet flows, projectile aerodynamics, high-speed combustion phenomena, and heat signature reduction and control. He is primarily an experimentalist engaging in wind/water tunnel testing while utilizing experimental techniques such as dye visualization, laser-induced fluorescence, schlieren visualization, force/moment measurements, hot-wire anemometry and in particular, particle-image velocimetry. He is also currently in the Advisory Committee of the International Symposium on Applications of Laser and Imaging Techniques to Fluid Mechanics (Lisbon Symposia). His publications appeared in Journal of Fluid Mechanics, Physical Review Fluids, Physics of Fluids, and Aerospace Science and Technology, among others, where some of his research work was selected for the covers of Journal of Fluid Mechanics and Physics of Fluids.
