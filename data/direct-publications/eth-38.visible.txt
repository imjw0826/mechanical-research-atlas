Membrane and Interfacial Science Laboratory: Overview and News
The Membrane and Interfacial Science Lab

Meet our interdisciplinary team advancing sustainable technologies for water, energy, and resource recovery.

Reinventing distillation

We design distillation systems that use ten times less energy.

Carbon capture and gas separations

We investigate membrane-based processes that enhance gas separations for climate and energy applications.

Transport in polymers

We fabricate nanoporous and polymer membranes, connecting structure and chemistry with separation performance.

Water purification

Our research develops systems to make purer water with less energy.

Sustainable separations by engineering membranes and interfaces

Our lab designs materials and processes that enable more energy-efficient and sustainable separations. We combine theory, materials synthesis, and experiments to enable high-performance technologies for water purification, gas separations, and resource recovery.
