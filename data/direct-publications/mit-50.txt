Bio

Sang-Gook Kim is a professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering.  He received his B.S. degree from Seoul National University (1978), M.S. from KAIST (1980), and Ph.D. from MIT (1985).  He held positions at Axiomatics Co., Cambridge, MA (1986) and Korea Institute of Science and Technology (1986-1991). Then he became a corporate executive director at Daewoo Corporation, Korea, and directed the Central Research Institute of Daewoo Electronics Co. until 2000 when he joined MIT. He is currently the Micro/Nano Area Head of the Department of Mechanical Engineering at MIT. Prof. Kim’s research has been in the field of product realization throughout his career at both the industry and academia. His recent research includes piezoelectric MEMS energy harvesting, micro ultrasonic transducers and nano-engineered energy conversion for carbon neutrality and solar water splitting systems. He is a fellow of CIRP (International Academy for Production Engineering), fellow of ASME, and overseas member of Korean National Academy of Engineering.

AI for Systems Design and Truely Smart Manufacturing

MNSL had been worked on how to better design and manufacture micro- and nano-scale functional systems including MEMS energy harvesters and actuators, solar water splitting for green hydrogen production (see the archived studies) until recently.

Right now our society is undergoing one of the most thrilling yet challenging transformations in its history due to the recent surge in deep learning and Generative AI. The current MNSL research aims to create a paradigm-shifting solution for the use of Generative AI in manufacturing systems to transform well-digitalized current manufacturing systems to become truly smart. The knowledge threads and know-hows in our Manufacturing-specific LLMs (MLLMs) will grow and evolve continually for future innovation and problem solving. Our research work will advance our understanding of knowledge threading (sharing and effective connectivity) in human-cyber-physical systems, as well as provide tools for manufacturing enterprises to achieve a significant boost in manufacturing productivity and long term leadership.  Our goal is to make Truly Smart Manufacturing systems.
