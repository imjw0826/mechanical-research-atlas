Biography

Dr. Zeynep Duygu Tekler is an Assistant Professor in AI for Sustainable and Intelligent Built Environments in the School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at Nanyang Technological University (NTU) Singapore.

Her research brings together artificial intelligence, building science, and human-centred systems to create sustainable built environments, spanning intelligent energy management, occupant-centric building operations, and predictive analytics. At NTU, her group at BASE Lab, develops scalable and adaptive systems that enable buildings to sense, learn, predict, and respond to changing environmental conditions and occupant needs, with the aim of improving energy efficiency, occupant health, and comfort.

Dr. Tekler received her Ph.D. in Engineering Product Development from the Singapore University of Technology and Design, followed by a Postdoctoral Research Fellowship at the National University of Singapore. Before joining NTU, she was a Principal Investigator and Senior Research Associate in the Department of Engineering Science at the University of Oxford, alongside a Research Fellowship at Kellogg College, University of Oxford. She was also appointed as Assistant Professor in AI for the Built Environment at Loughborough University.
