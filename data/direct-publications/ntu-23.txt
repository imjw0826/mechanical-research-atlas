Biography

Prof. KIM Mun Sek joined the School of Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering (MAE) at Nanyang Technological University (NTU) in October 2025 as a Nanyang Assistant Professor.

He obtained his Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering from Stanford University (2019–2024), an M.S. from Cornell University (2014–2016), and a B.S. from the University of California, Berkeley (2010–2014). During his national military service, he served as a Research Scientist at the Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST) in Seoul, South Korea (2016–2019). Prior to joining NTU MAE, he was a Senior Scientist at SES AI in the United States (2024–2025). His research focuses on battery interphases, electrolytes, and nanomaterials for next-generation energy storage systems.

He is committed to translating fundamental electrochemical principles into practical technologies and to mentoring the next generation of engineers and researchers. At NTU, he leads MSK LAB, which focuses on pioneering research that integrates electrochemistry, battery materials, and AI-driven innovations to advance energy storage solutions that are both transformative and practical.
