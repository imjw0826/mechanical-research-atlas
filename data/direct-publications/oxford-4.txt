Biography

Natalia Ares works on experiments to advance the development of quantum technologies, with a focus on artificial intelligence for quantum device control and quantum thermodynamics. She joined the Materials Department at the University of Oxford in 2013. She was awarded a series of fellowships, including a Marie Skłodowska-Curie and a Royal Society University Research Fellowship, and was awarded a European Research Council Starting Grant in 2020. During her PhD she focused on silicon-based devices for quantum computing at CEA Grenoble, France. She completed her undergraduate studies in Physics and a Masters equivalent in the theory of quantum chaos at the University of Buenos Aires, Argentina, where she was born and raised.

Quantum Device Lab, Department of Engineering Science, University of Oxford
Quantum Device Lab
Welcome to Natalia Ares' Group Page

We research quantum behaviour in nanoscale devices. The manipulation of nanoscale devices in the quantum regime has gone from proof-of-principle experiments to being the building block of a new generation of quantum technologies...it is an exciting time for quantum research!

Philanthropists

We have benefited enormously from the support of Sir Martin Wood FRS and Lady Audrey Wood, the founders of OI, who have sadly passed away. Their timely donation led to greatly improved nanofabrication facilities at Oxford. Our research continues to rely heavily on an electron beam lithography system which they helped to establish.

Vital pump-priming for our low-temperature activities came from a major donation from the Ferreras Willetts family, who have continued to support us generously. We are proud to acknowledge this involvement by naming our laboratories the Ferreras Willetts Laboratories.
