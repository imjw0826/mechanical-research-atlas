Human-centered Sensing Laboratory

: Overview and News
Welcome to the Human-centered Sensing Lab!

We work at the interface between chemistry, physics and engineering to equip next-generation electronics with the sense of smell.

Surfaces for Molecular Sensing

We investigate interactions between molecules and solid-state surfaces developing techniques for their in-situ chracterization. Our particular interest lies in phenomena that trigger measurable signals (electrical and optical) to be exploited for sensing applications.

Micro- and Nanoscaled Systems

To overcome frontiers in molecular sensing, we engineer processes to fabricate materials at the nanoscale with tailor-made surface chemistries. Their self-assembly to porous sensing films with controlled diffusion-reaction properties and interfacing with micromachined structures yiels high performance sensor systems.

Medical Diagnostics

We develop wearable tools based on our sensing concepts that enable the detection of critical biomarkers from breath or interstitial fluid minimally invasive at the point-of-care. In close collaboration with our clinical partners, we strive to enable precision diagnostics and therapies.

Sustainability

Ranging from air quality monitoring, over the detection of contaminants in food, to the safe handling of green fuels (e.g., hydrogen, ammonia, methanol), our lab develops tools to detect and quantify critical molecules at trace-level concentrations.

Human-centered Sensing Laboratory

Our group works on molecular sensing integrating concepts of chemistry, physics and engineering. We offer a unique bouquet of fundamental and applied research on sensor technologies to respond to societal needs.
