Biography

He earned his B.Sc. degree in Mechanical Engineering and his M.Sc. degree in System Dynamics and Control from Istanbul Technical University, Turkey, in 2008 and 2010, respectively. In 2014, he completed his Ph.D. at KU Leuven, Belgium. Dr. Kayacan has held academic positions at the University of Oklahoma, the University of Queensland, the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, and the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.

Dr. Erkan Kayacan has joined Nanyang Technological University as an Assistant Professor in Intelligent Robotics and Autonomous Systems since 2025. His research focuses on real-time optimization-based algorithms and physics-informed machine learning, with a strong emphasis on applications in autonomous systems and robotics.

Dr. Kayacan received the Best Systems Paper Award at the Robotics: Science and Systems (RSS) conference in 2018. He currently serves as a Technical Editor for the IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics.
