SHORT BIO: 

Dr Huangwei Zhang is an associate professor in Department of Mechanical Engineering at National University of Singapore (NUS). He received his Bachelor’s and Master’s degree in Engineering Mechanics and Fluid Mechanics from Beihang University (China) in 2007 and 2009 respectively, Ph.D. degree in Combustion from University of Cambridge (United Kingdom) in 2016. Dr Zhang is the Fellow of the Royal Aeronautical Society and the recipient of 2026 Hiroshi Tsuji Early Career Researcher Award from The Combustion Institute. 

Dr Zhang’s research focuses on advancing low-carbon combustion and energy conversion technologies to support the global transition toward sustainable energy. His work combines fundamental combustion science, advanced laser diagnostics, computational modelling, and system integration to develop next-generation power and propulsion systems. Dr Zhang leads several multidisciplinary research projects in collaboration with universities, research institutes, and industry partners. His recent work has focused on ammonia-fuelled gas turbines, integrated ammonia cracking–combustion systems, low-emission combustion technologies, and advanced optical diagnostics for turbulent flames. He also applies combustion modelling to safety-critical problems, including hydrogen explosion hazards and lithium-ion battery thermal runaway and fire behavior. This broad scope reflects a consistent theme in his work: improving both the performance and safety of emerging energy technologies.

Dr Zhang is the Chair or co-Chair of the organizing or programme committees of leading international combustion conferences, including the 15th Asia-Pacific Conference on Combustion in 2025 (ASPACC 2025) and The International Colloquium on the Dynamics of Explosions and Reactive Systems in 2027 (ICDERS 2027). He is on the Editorial Board for the numerous journals, e.g., Combustion and Flame. He was the guest editor of the inaugural odd-year issue of Proceedings of the Combustion Institute for ASPACC 2025. He serves as the Chair of the Singapore Section of the Combustion Institute  (SSCI), on the Board of Directors of The Institute for Dynamics of Explosions and Reactive Systems (IDERS), and Programme Director of MSc in Robotics in NUS Department of Mechanical Engineering.
