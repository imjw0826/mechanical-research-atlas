Prof.dr. V.A. (Vera) Popovich
Prof.dr. V.A. (Vera) Popovich
Profiel

Vera Popovich is a full Professor of Materials and Mechanics in Extreme Conditions at TU Delft’s Materials Science and Engineering department. Her work sits at the crossroads of materials science, multiscale mechanics, and advanced manufacturing. Her research group investigates how materials perform and fail under severe environments — including hydrogen embrittlement, cryogenic temperatures, and the extreme conditions found in aerospace and high‑tech applications.

She leads the Extreme Materials & Mechanics (X2M) group, integrating in‑situ experiments, multiscale modelling, fracture mechanics, and additive manufacturing to develop materials that are more predictable, reliable, and long‑lasting. She also oversees two laboratories — the Multiscale Mechanical Behaviour Laboratory and the Materials for Additive Manufacturing Laboratory — and is actively involved in engineering education, coordinating several bachelor’s and master’s courses.

Expertise:

Fracture and fatigue of materials
Extreme conditions 
Design of materials through additive manufacturing
Additively manufactured smart materials
Functionally graded materials
High and low temperature mechanical properties
Microstructure-damage evolution
Hydrogen Embrittlement and degradation
