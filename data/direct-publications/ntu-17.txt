Biography

Hortense Le Ferrand is associate professor at the School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering in joint appointment with the School of Materials Science and Engineering. Her research group combines sustainable manufacturing with biomimetic/bioinspired designs to develop high performance materials. Specifically, the processing methods use additive manufacturing, abundant building blocks, and water as a solvent. These technologies permit the fabrication of composites with complex bioinspired microstructural designs leading to unusual combinations of properties. These composites can be used in a variety of applications: thermal management in electronics or architecture, biomedical implants, protective ceramics, sustainable composites, etc.

Hortense received her Ingénieur Degree from the Ecole Supérieure de Physique et de Chimie Industrielle de la ville de Paris (ESPCI Paris, France) and her MSc and PhD in Materials Science from ETH Zürich (Switzerland). She was a visiting scholar at the school of Mechanical Engineering at Purdue University (USA) and postdoctoral visiting scholar at the Center for Biomimetic Sensor Science at NTU before joining the faculty of NTU in 2019.

In 2020, Hortense received the prestigious Nanyang Research Foundation (NRF) Fellowship to continue research on additive manufacturing of bioinspired composites and became Nanyang Assistant Professor (NAP). Since 2023, Hortense is co-chair of Women@NTU and POWERS (for the Promotion of Women in Engineering, Research and Science). Since May 2025, Hortense is serving as Assistant Dean (Graduate) at the College of Engineering.
