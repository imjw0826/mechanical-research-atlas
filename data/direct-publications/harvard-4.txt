ABOUT
Computing + AI … for all Humankind

We strive to understand Nature and to solve problems in Science and Engineering through the fusion of models, algorithms, data and Artificial Intelligence. In this fusion, we value critical thinking, emotions and ethics. We define our work at the interface of  Computing and AI.

We believe that Computing + AI is essential for all Humankind.
